Who we are

A space dedicated to healing
a place to pause, reconnect
and live close to nature.

Filled with olive trees, a vegetable garden, animals, and the sound of the sea in the background, we value slow, eco living. Here, daily life is shaped by simple things:

movement & breath working the land shared meals quiet moments human connection

Guest stays · Glamping

Quiet, nature,
and time to yourself

We host guests in a simple and intimate way - solo travelers, couples or small groups who appreciate simplicity, nature, and a slower pace.

You stay in a private glamping tipi, comfortable and fully equipped, with access to a shared kitchen for cooking delicious vegetarian meals.

Join the weekly yoga lessons, swim at beautiful beaches, visit nearby villages and hot springs, learn to work the land - or simply rest. Whether you come for a few days or longer, the invitation is simple: slow down, breathe, and just be.
